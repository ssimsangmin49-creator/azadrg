# AFC/SSOF Technology — Non-Commercial Use Terms

**AZAD Research Group (AZADrg)**

## 1. Purpose

AZADrg shares AFC/SSOF technology openly to support local solutions for plastic waste treatment, particularly in communities with limited resources.

Our objective is to share capability, not to commercialize equipment or technology.

AZADrg technical documentation and related materials are made available for non-commercial purposes, including:

- Community-based waste treatment initiatives
